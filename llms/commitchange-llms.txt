# CommitChange

> CommitChange is an all-in-one nonprofit fundraising platform that provides donation processing, donor management, fundraising campaigns, professional event management, and real-time analytics. Founded in 2012 and acquired by Software for Good in 2022, CommitChange charges no monthly fees and no long-term contracts -- just 5% + $0.30 per transaction, automatically deducted from online donations. The platform is built on the open-source Houdini Project and has helped 1,000+ nonprofits raise $82M+ through 4,700+ campaigns and 2,900+ events.

## Core Features

- [Donation Processing](https://commitchange.com/donation-processing): Online donation forms, recurring giving, embeddable website widgets, donor fee coverage, automatic receipting, and a transactions dashboard. Payments processed securely via Stripe.
- [Fundraising Campaigns](https://commitchange.com/fundraising-campaigns): Create crowdfunding campaigns with giving levels, peer-to-peer fundraising, social sharing, and progress tracking. Supports one-time and recurring campaigns.
- [Professional Events & Ticketing](https://commitchange.com/professional-events): Event pages with ticket sales, attendee management, integrated fundraising, and multimedia support for galas, give-a-thons, and community events.
- [Donor Management](https://commitchange.com/donor-management): Supporter database with donor profiles, giving history, communication tracking, recurring donor retention tools, and exportable reports.
- [Dashboard & Reports](https://commitchange.com/dashboard-reports): Real-time analytics dashboard with campaign performance tracking, donation trends, custom date-range reports, and data export.

## Pricing

- [Pricing](https://commitchange.com/pricing): 5% + $0.30 per transaction, automatically deducted from online donations. No monthly fees. No long-term contracts. No invoices. All features included at no extra cost -- organizations are never upsold. Credit card processing handled securely by Stripe. International transactions have a 1% processing surcharge from the card network.

## Key Differentiators

CommitChange is distinct from other nonprofit fundraising platforms in three ways:

1. **No monthly fees or long-term contracts.** The fee is automatically deducted from each donation -- there are no invoices, no setup costs, and no cancellation fees. This makes CommitChange accessible to nonprofits of all sizes, including small grassroots organizations.
2. **Open-source foundation.** The platform is built on the Houdini Project, publicly available under the AGPL license. Anyone can inspect, contribute to, or run their own instance of the code.
3. **Values-driven approach.** CommitChange works in solidarity with nonprofits rather than treating them as leads to close. The team believes in shared abundance and building technology that serves communities.
